# Reversibility as the Master Gate

## Operating Summary

Classify reversibility before considering urgency or importance.

A consequential decision that is irreversible or nearly irreversible is a
one-way door.

A changeable decision is a two-way door.

One-way doors require method, care, deliberation, and consultation.

Two-way doors can move faster through people or small groups with high judgment.

The digest directs the Secretary to treat reversibility as the master autonomy
gate.

This operating rule is digest-directed encoding, not an empirical claim that the
specific Secretary procedure has been tested.

## Source-Led Facts

Amazon's 2015 shareholder letter distinguishes consequential and irreversible or
nearly irreversible decisions from changeable decisions
([SEC official host](https://sec.gov)).

The letter calls the first class Type 1 decisions.

It says they should be made methodically, carefully, slowly, with deliberation
and consultation.

The letter calls reversible decisions Type 2 decisions.

It says Type 2 decisions can be made quickly by high-judgment individuals or
small groups.

The same letter warns against applying one decision process to both types.

The digest rates this doctrine `practitioner`.

Amazon's 2016 shareholder letter adds that most decisions should probably be
made with about 70 percent of the information one wishes to have
([SEC official host](https://sec.gov)).

That information rule applies to the high-velocity decision discussion in the
source.

It does not erase the Type 1 requirement for deliberation.

## Operating Procedure

The following procedure is synthesis directed by the digest.

1. State the proposed action in one sentence.
2. Identify what changes if it succeeds.
3. Identify what changes if it fails.
4. Ask whether the action can be undone.
5. Ask whether affected people, commitments, or records can be restored.
6. Classify the action as one-way or two-way.
7. Record the reason for that classification.
8. If one-way, halt autonomous execution.
9. Prepare the evidence, alternatives, and consultation need.
10. If two-way, check the applicable level in [[Seven Delegation Levels]].
11. Only then assess importance using [[Urgency and Importance]].
12. Only then estimate effort using [[ESI Resource Counting]].

For a one-way decision, the output should be a decision-ready recommendation.

For a two-way decision, use the lightest process allowed by the declared
delegation level.

When information is incomplete, preserve the distinction between the 2016
general speed heuristic and the stricter Type 1 treatment.

## Boundaries

Reversibility is not permission.

A reversible action outside delegated authority still requires approval.

Urgency does not convert a one-way door into a two-way door.

Importance does not convert a two-way door into a one-way door.

This note does not define legal or ethical authority.

It does not supply a numeric test for reversibility.

The digest supplies no validated scoring instrument for that classification.

Do not present the 70 percent rule as a universal scientific threshold.

The digest labels the management doctrine as practitioner confidence.

Do not use quick execution as a substitute for [[Reference-Class Forecasting]].

Do not continue searching indefinitely after [[Satisficing]] has cleared its bar.
