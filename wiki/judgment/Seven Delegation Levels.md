# Seven Delegation Levels

## Operating Summary

Represent autonomy as a seven-value enum for each decision category.

The levels are Tell, Sell, Consult, Agree, Advise, Inquire, and Delegate.

The level is negotiated per decision category.

It is not a global property of the secretary.

A level for scheduling does not silently transfer to spending, correspondence,
or commitments.

The digest rates this model `practitioner`.

Reversibility still dominates the execution gate.

## Source-Led Facts

Management 3.0 publishes the seven-level list and ties it to key decision areas ([primary locator](https://management30.com/empower-teams/delegation-empowerment/)).

The digest attributes seven delegation levels to Appelo's Management 3.0.

Level 1 is Tell.

Level 2 is Sell.

Level 3 is Consult.

Level 4 is Agree.

Level 5 is Advise.

Level 6 is Inquire.

Level 7 is Delegate.

The digest states that the level is negotiated per decision category, not
globally.

The digest does not supply an exact primary URL, so the official locator above was verified separately.

## Operating Procedure

The following procedure is synthesis directed by the digest.

1. Name the decision category precisely.
2. Retrieve the declared level for that category.
3. If no level exists, report `no data` for delegated authority.
4. Do not infer a level from adjacent categories.
5. Classify reversibility using [[Reversibility as the Master Gate]].
6. Preserve the exact stored enum value in the decision record.
7. Do not silently promote a category to a higher-numbered level.
8. Do not silently demote a category to a lower-numbered level.
9. Do not infer behavior for the label beyond what the profile declares.
10. If the profile gives only the label, expose that interpretive gap.
11. Ask for the missing category rule before consequential execution.
12. Keep the category and the enum value together.
13. Record the level used with the decision outcome.
14. Escalate any mismatch between the requested action and the stored category.

Use [[Urgency and Importance]] for priority, not for authority.

Use [[ESI Resource Counting]] for resolution effort, not for authority.

## Boundaries

The enum represents degrees of delegation.

It does not supersede law, ethics, contract, or the Secretary authority boundary.

Level 7 does not make an irreversible act safe.

Level 7 in one category does not grant Level 7 elsewhere.

Silence is not a stored level.

Repeated action is not a substitute for explicit category negotiation.

The digest does not provide a validated performance comparison among the seven
levels.

The digest lists the names and order but does not define the operational conduct
associated with each label.

Do not present the model as evidence-based.

Do not expand the claims from the locator beyond the digest.

Do not use [[Satisficing]] to manufacture an authority level.

Do not use [[Reference-Class Forecasting]] to infer permission from past action.
