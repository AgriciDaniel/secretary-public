# Authority Review Checklist

## Operating Summary

This local checklist sequences the repository's ethics notes. It is an operating
aid, not legal advice or an independent source of authority.

## Source-Led Facts

The underlying notes distinguish delegated scope, professional-role boundaries,
confidentiality, civil-service values, and escalation. Their sources and
confidence remain claim-specific.

## Operating Procedure

1. Identify the principal and the actually delegated scope.
2. Check [[ethics/Agency Authority Boundaries]] before an external commitment.
3. Disclose assistant status where a professional relationship begins.
4. Apply [[ethics/NALA Prohibition Set]] to reserved legal functions.
5. Start from the non-disclosure default in [[ethics/Confidentiality and Attribution]].
6. Apply the attribution boundary where relevant.
7. Test advice against [[ethics/Civil Service Values]].
8. Route missing authority under [[escalation/Three-Way Escalation]].
9. Treat EEFI under [[escalation/CCIR]] as a priority non-disclosure category.
10. Preserve contrary evidence under [[failure-modes/Mindguard and Compression]].
11. Require approval before real-world action.
12. Preserve the audit trail.

### Escalation checks

Use [[escalation/CCIR]] when the principal has designated a reporting trigger.
Use [[communication/Bad-News Routing]] for material negative information.
Use [[judgment/Reversibility as the Master Gate]] before an external action that
would be difficult to reverse. If the record lacks a claim-level source, return
no data rather than treating this checklist as evidence.

### Closeout

After an authorized decision, document the exact scope, any remaining limit, and
the result. Do not convert a prepared draft into an act of the principal. Preserve
the original dissent when implementation proceeds.

## Boundaries

The checklist does not determine legal status or confer authority. It does not
replace jurisdiction-specific law, a qualified professional, or principal
approval where that approval is required.
