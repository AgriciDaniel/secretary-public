# Correspondence SLA Regime

## Operating Summary

Use the UK Cabinet Office regime as a documented correspondence workflow.
It is an institutional example, not a universal deadline for every principal.
It separates correspondence signed by a minister from correspondence signed by the office.
Its control value lies in explicit clocks, ownership, transfer, holding-reply, and closure rules.
The source is the September 2024 Guide to Handling Correspondence (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).

## Source-Led Facts

The regime has two tiers: ministerial and public or official (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
The principal signs ministerial correspondence (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
The office signs public or official correspondence (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
The target must not exceed 20 working days (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
The first working day is the day after receipt (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
A substantive reply answers all or most points or questions raised (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
A holding reply does not clear the case (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
A holding reply does not stop or reset the original clock (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
It must apologise, explain, and state when the substantive reply will come (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
A transfer request is due within one working day (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
After acceptance, the receiving department has three working days for ministerial correspondence (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
After acceptance, it has five working days for public correspondence (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
Responsibility remains with the transferring department until acceptance (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
A transfer may not be refused on GDPR grounds (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
Declining a reply is limited to documented grounds with named approval authority (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
The listed grounds include offensive language (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
They include illegibility, selling a product, information only, foreign language, and vexatious correspondence (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
The editorial mantra is accuracy, brevity, and clarity (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).
The digest reports 181,927 items received in 2025, up 31 percent, with 129,167 on time, or 71 percent (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/handling-government-correspondence-guidance).

## Operating Procedure

The following is a direct adaptation of the documented controls.

1. Record receipt time and source.
2. Set day one to the next working day.
3. Classify the signing tier.
4. Record the accountable owner.
5. Record the substantive deadline.
6. Identify every question raised.
7. Draft for all or most questions to meet the substantive-reply test.
8. If delay is unavoidable, draft a holding reply.
9. In the holding reply, apologise.
10. Explain the delay.
11. State when the substantive reply will come.
12. Preserve the original deadline in the tracker.
13. If transfer is needed, request it within one working day.
14. Keep ownership until acceptance is recorded.
15. After acceptance, apply the receiving-time rule appropriate to the tier.
16. Do not use data-protection language as an unsupported transfer refusal.
17. If recommending no reply, name the documented ground.
18. Name the approval authority for that ground.
19. Route missing authority through [[escalation/Three-Way Escalation]].
20. Draft using the controls in [[communication/BLUF and Numeric Writing Constraints]].
21. Use [[communication/Bad-News Routing]] when the substance is adverse.
22. Preserve legal or publication conditions through [[communication/No-Surprises Protocol]].

## Boundaries

The 20-working-day maximum belongs to the cited Cabinet Office regime.
Do not assert that it governs a different organization without adoption.
The digest does not provide a generic SLA for private correspondence.
The digest does not provide an automated working-day calendar.
Compute dates deterministically outside the model when this workflow is implemented.
A holding reply is not substantive closure.
A transfer request is not transfer acceptance.
Office signing authority is profile-specific and cannot be inferred from this doctrine.
Use [[roles/Completed Staff Work and Authority]] before implying that the secretary may sign.
Use [[judgment/Reversibility as the Master Gate]] for consequential exceptions.
