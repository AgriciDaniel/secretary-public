# Paper Flow and Calendar Horizons

## Operating Summary

Use the paper-flow examples to design visibility and sequencing, not to prescribe a universal pack.
The UK red box example shows a mixed overnight workload.
The White House Book example adds a daily calendar, a three-week view, and a three-month view.
The digest identifies that three-horizon display as the most encodable time-horizon artefact found.
It places the quarter before the principal daily at near-zero additional attention cost (https://whitehousetransitionproject.org).

## Operating Procedure

The following procedure is synthesis from the two institutional examples.

1. Separate material requiring signature from material requiring a decision.
2. Separate drafts from final artefacts.
3. Separate invitations from correspondence.
4. Separate public lines from private advice.
5. Separate briefing material from source material.
6. Keep an explicit immediate lane.
7. Do not let the immediate lane erase the ordinary queue.
8. Show today's detailed calendar.
9. Show a three-week planning view.
10. Show a three-month planning view.
11. Link each significant future item to [[escalation/Branches and Sequels]].
12. Link principal-designated decision triggers to [[escalation/CCIR]].
13. Use [[communication/I-PASS Briefing Schema]] for handoffs.
14. Use [[communication/Ministerial Decision Memo]] for decision papers.
15. Preserve dissent during compression through [[failure-modes/Mindguard and Compression]].
16. Track what is for information only.
17. Track what requires a response.
18. Track what requires principal signature.
19. Track what requires office action.
20. Keep the source document linked to any summary.
21. Present the quarter without pretending that the calendar alone is a strategy.
22. Audit the calendar using [[duties/CEO Time-Use Baseline]].

## Boundaries

The red box counts are an example of real load, not quota or ideal composition.
The digest does not provide a standard red-box assembly procedure.
The digest does not provide a primary briefing-pack format.
Use [[gaps/No Primary Briefing Pack Format]] rather than inventing one.
The three-week and three-month horizons are institutional artefacts, not evidence of causal effectiveness.
The Book's preparation frequency does not establish a requirement for every office.
This note does not authorize schedule changes.
This note does not define priority solely by paper type.
Use [[duties/Duty Taxonomy and Priority]] to keep communication and writing central.
Use [[judgment/Reversibility as the Master Gate]] before routing consequential items.
