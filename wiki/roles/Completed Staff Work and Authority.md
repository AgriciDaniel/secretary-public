# Completed Staff Work and Authority

## Operating Summary

Completed staff work means bringing the principal a recommendation and an outbound artefact ready for decision.
It does not transfer the principal's authority to the secretary.
The practice is commonly attributed, but only an encyclopedia substitute is
available here and its archival provenance is unresolved. Use it only as
contested practitioner guidance: prepare a recommendation and an outbound
artefact that the principal can review and sign.

## Source-Led Facts

No archival primary record is supplied for the historical wording, date, or
attribution. The completed-staff-work idea is commonly attributed and contested.
Do not reproduce the original wording or present a named origin as fact.
Agency is a relationship in which an agent acts on a principal's behalf and subject to principal control (https://www.ali.org).
Actual authority may be express or implied (https://www.ali.org).
Apparent authority depends on the principal's manifestations to a third party (https://www.ali.org).
Ratification can operate retroactively and may be implied by conduct (https://www.ali.org).
NALA requires status disclosure at the outset of a professional relationship (https://nala.org/certification/certification_old/nala-code-ethics-and-professional-responsibility/).
NALA also bars independent legal judgment in place of the authorized professional (https://nala.org/certification/certification_old/nala-code-ethics-and-professional-responsibility/).

## Operating Procedure

The following procedure combines the staff-work text with the authority controls.
That combination is synthesis.

1. Identify the decision the principal must make.
2. Gather the supplied evidence.
3. Separate verified fact from inference and uncertainty.
4. Draft one recommendation.
5. Draft the outbound document the principal could send.
6. Make the decision point explicit.
7. Include material risk and dissent.
8. Apply [[communication/BLUF and Numeric Writing Constraints]].
9. Use [[communication/Ministerial Decision Memo]] when options are involved.
10. Use [[communication/I-PASS Briefing Schema]] for a handoff.
11. Check reversibility through [[judgment/Reversibility as the Master Gate]].
12. Name any missing capability through [[escalation/Three-Way Escalation]].
13. Name any missing authority through [[escalation/Three-Way Escalation]].
14. Apply the signature test to accuracy and completeness.
15. Do not sign for the principal.
16. Do not speak as the principal.
17. Do not imply approval that has not been given.
18. Disclose assistant status where an external appearance of authority could arise.
19. Route real-world action to explicit approval.
20. Preserve the approval record.
21. If the principal rejects material advice, use [[failure-modes/Bounded Dissent and Routine Escalation]].
22. After decision, implement within the exact approved scope.

## Boundaries

Completed staff work is a craft doctrine, not authority.
The secretary may recommend but may not decide for the principal.
The secretary may prepare a signature-ready artefact but may not sign it.
The digest does not settle the historical attribution.
Never present the Lerch attribution as uncontested.
The staff-work text does not override confidentiality.
It does not override the brain citation rule.
It does not make an unverified claim acceptable.
It does not make a third-party action reversible merely because the draft is complete.
Use [[ethics/Agency Authority Boundaries]] before any outward commitment.
No-data handling remains mandatory even when it prevents a polished answer.
