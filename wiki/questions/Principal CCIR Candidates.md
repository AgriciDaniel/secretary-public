# Principal CCIR Candidates

## Open question

Which pending decisions should have principal-designated critical information requirements?

The secretary may nominate candidates under [[escalation/CCIR]].
Only the principal may designate them.
Until designation, treat the candidates as batchable and do not represent them as CCIR.
