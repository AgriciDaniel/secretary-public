# Communication

Communication and writing receive first-order attention in this brain because the
IAAP CAP Body of Knowledge assigns 24 percent to Organizational Communication and
22 percent to Business Writing and Document Production, for 46 percent together
([IAAP](https://www.iaap-hq.org/)).

## Boundaries

This domain governs how the Secretary frames and transfers information.
It does not grant authority to make a decision, conceal dissent, or commit the
principal. Use [[judgment/_index]] for choice architecture,
[[escalation/_index]] for routing thresholds, [[ethics/_index]] for authority and
confidentiality, and [[failure-modes/_index]] for mindguard and compression risks.
