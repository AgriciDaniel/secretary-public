# BLUF and Numeric Writing Constraints

## Operating Summary

Put the main point at the beginning and use active voice. Then test the writing
against the numeric constraints recorded from AR 25-50 and DA Pam 600-67
([Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/)).

The operating target is comprehension in one rapid reading. The numbers are gates
for revision, not permission to remove material dissent or uncertainty.

The clarity index is average sentence length plus the percentage of words having
three or more syllables. The target is 30
([Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/)).

## Source-Led Facts

- AR 25-50 says effective Army writing is understood in a single rapid reading
  ([Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/)).
- It requires the main point at the beginning, described as bottom line up front
  ([Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/)).
- It requires active voice
  ([Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/)).
- Average sentence length should be about 15 words
  ([Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/)).
- Paragraphs should be no more than 10 lines
  ([Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/)).
- Most letters and memorandums should fit on one page
  ([Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/)).
- The source advises avoiding sentences that begin with “It is,” “There is,” or
  “There are” ([Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/)).
- DA Pam 600-67 defines the clarity index as average sentence length plus the
  percentage of words of three or more syllables
  ([Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/)).
- The target clarity index is 30
  ([Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/)).
- A score below 20 reads too abruptly, while a score above 40 is difficult to
  understand ([Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/)).

## Operating Procedure

### 1. Write the bottom line

- State the decision, finding, or requested action in the first sentence.
- For a decision paper, align the opening with
  [[communication/Ministerial Decision Memo]].
- For bad news, do not use a long opening to delay the message. Apply
  [[communication/Bad-News Routing]].
- Keep the difference between fact, inference, and uncertainty visible.

### 2. Prefer active voice

- Name the actor where the evidence identifies one.
- Name the action.
- Name the object or consequence.
- Do not invent an actor merely to satisfy the form.
- Preserve uncertainty where the source does not establish responsibility.

### 3. Measure sentence length

- Compute the average words per sentence.
- Compare the result with the about-15-word source target.
- Shorten or split dense sentences while preserving meaning.
- Do not force every sentence to exactly 15 words.
- Recheck after revision.

### 4. Measure paragraph length

- Count rendered lines in the intended format where that format is available.
- Revise paragraphs over the 10-line source maximum.
- Keep each paragraph focused on one operating purpose.
- Do not separate a caveat from the claim it qualifies.

### 5. Apply the page target

- Aim for one page for most letters and memorandums.
- Keep a principal-facing decision summary aligned with
  [[communication/Ministerial Decision Memo]].
- Move supporting detail only when the main page remains accurate.
- Never move the decisive risk or dissent out of the principal's reading path.

### 6. Calculate the clarity index

- Calculate average sentence length.
- Calculate the percentage of words with three or more syllables.
- Add those two values.
- Revise toward 30.
- Treat a result below 20 as too abrupt under the source guidance.
- Treat a result above 40 as difficult under the source guidance.
- Recalculate after edits.

### 7. Run the opening-pattern check

- Find sentences beginning “It is.”
- Find sentences beginning “There is.”
- Find sentences beginning “There are.”
- Rewrite them where an active and accurate construction is available.
- Retain one only when revision would reduce accuracy.

### 8. Review the handoff

- Use [[communication/I-PASS Briefing Schema]] when the document transfers action.
- Use [[communication/No-Surprises Protocol]] when early notification is required.
- Use [[escalation/_index]] if the message identifies missing authority or capability.
- Use [[failure-modes/_index]] to check that compression preserved dissent.

## Boundaries

- Numeric compliance does not establish that a claim is true.
- A clarity score does not replace a human decision about meaning.
- The about-15-word figure is an average, not a sentence-by-sentence requirement.
- The one-page target applies to most letters and memorandums, not every artifact.
- A short document can still conceal bad news.
- Active voice may not be used to assign responsibility unsupported by evidence.
- Use [[judgment/_index]] for decision quality, not this writing gate.
